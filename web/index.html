Findings, review steps and legal basis

Pillar Two screening

Transitional CbCR Safe Harbour: de minimis (revenue < €10m and profit < €1m), simplified ETR (≥ 15% for 2023–24, 16% for 2025, 17% for 2026–27), routine profits (profit ≤ substance-based income exclusion). Estimated top-up = (15% − ETR) × (profit − SBIE). ? = needs payroll costs and tangible assets; ≤ = upper bound. A screening estimate from CbCR figures, not a GloBE computation. Amounts assumed in EUR.

Method and data

Risk score = 50% rule-based indicators + 30% logistic-regression probability + 20% k-means anomaly score. High ≥ 60, Medium ≥ 35. The model never sees the jurisdiction's name; it learns the financial profile of multinationals' tax-haven affiliates, so it can flag that profile anywhere.

Data: EU Tax Observatory, Public Country-by-Country Reports database (Aliprandi, Borders, Gabriel & von Zedlitz, 2022; non-commercial use with citation) · OECD Corporate Tax Statistics, aggregated CbCR 2016 · EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions, Annex I as of 9 October 2026.

Input columns: jurisdiction, revenue, profit_before_tax, tax_paid or tax_accrued, employees; optional group, year, tangible_assets, payroll_costs.

TaxRiskScan produces risk indicators, not legal or tax conclusions. A high score means "look closer", not "non-compliant": low effective tax rates and high profits can have legitimate explanations. Every flag needs human review.
