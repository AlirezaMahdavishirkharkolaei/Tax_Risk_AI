# Data sources

TaxRiskScan's code is MIT-licensed. The bundled data files keep the terms of their original publishers.

## 1. Training data: EU Tax Observatory, Public Country-by-Country Reports database

* **File:** `taxriskscan/data/training_public_cbcr.csv` (5,004 jurisdiction rows, 96 multinationals, 237 reports, fiscal years 2015–2023, amounts in EUR)
* **What it is:** the country-by-country reports that large multinationals have published voluntarily, collected and standardised by the EU Tax Observatory. The same data powers its Taxplorer tool. Each jurisdiction is tagged as a tax haven or not; TaxRiskScan uses this tag as the training label.
* **Citation (requested by the publisher):** Aliprandi, G., Borders, K., Gabriel, F., von Zedlitz, G. (2022): "Public Country-by-Country Reports: a new database", EU Tax Observatory. Documentation: <https://www.taxobservatory.eu/www-site/uploads/2023/02/Public_CbCRs_dataset_documentation-2.pdf>
* **Terms:** free for **non-commercial use** with the citation above. Any commercial use of the trained model needs your own data or permission from the EU Tax Observatory.
* **Copy used:** `dataviz/data/data_final_dataviz.csv` from <https://github.com/dataforgoodfr/12_taxobservatory> (Data For Good × EU Tax Observatory), commit `966b359a66c963ddeb031612ee897b2e0aa81708`
* **Processing** (`scripts/build_real_datasets.py`):
  * removed "Other / rest of world" rows and rows without profit, employees, revenue or any tax figure
  * kept only reports covering at least 3 jurisdictions

## 2. Benchmarks: OECD Corporate Tax Statistics, aggregated CbCR (Table I), fiscal year 2016

* **File:** `taxriskscan/data/oecd_cbcr_2016_benchmarks.csv` (164 jurisdictions with at least 5 reports)
* **What it is:** for each jurisdiction, totals across all reporting countries of the profit, tax accrued and employees of multinationals' sub-groups located there. From these the file derives the ETR of profitable sub-groups and the ratio of profit share to employee share.
* **Source:** OECD, Corporate Tax Statistics, dataset `CBCR_TABLEI` (OECD.Stat, amounts in USD). Downloaded 9 Nov 2020 by F. Heitmüller and published at <https://github.com/fheitmueller/cbcrdata> (commit `79a7eb4790604aecc8058db099a35c2a868a0c52`).
* **Terms:** OECD data may be reused with attribution (OECD Terms and Conditions).
* **Use:** context only. These figures are shown next to each entity and do not affect the score.

## 3. Reference lists (`taxriskscan/jurisdictions.py`)

* **EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions (Annex I),** as revised by the Council on 9 October 2026
* **Jurisdictions with no or nominal general corporate income tax:** a hand-maintained list, used as a weak indicator

## 4. Synthetic data

`taxriskscan/data/training_cbcr.csv` is generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py`. It is used for tests and as an optional demo model (`--train synthetic`).

## Rebuilding the real-data files

```bash
git clone https://github.com/dataforgoodfr/12_taxobservatory
git clone https://github.com/fheitmueller/cbcrdata
python scripts/build_real_datasets.py \
  --taxobs 12_taxobservatory/dataviz/data/data_final_dataviz.csv \
  --oecd cbcrdata/data_raw/CBCR_TABLEI.csv
```
